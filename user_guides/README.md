# Welcome to CrossWars — a free, social, mini-crossword

## What is CrossWars?
CrossWars is a free web app that delivers a daily mini-crossword and adds real-time social competition: solve solo or race friends while seeing each other’s progress. If you like mini-crosswords and want the option to race against friends for who can complete the daily mini, you're in the right place!

## Overview

- A free daily mini-crossword experience.
   - AI-assisted puzzle generation
- Play solo or race a friend.
- View your stats.
- Clear, simple yet attractive design.

## Features
- Solo and Battle crosswords are generated once daily! The word list is generated with an LLM and a radomized them, then words are chosen and made into a crossword grid.
- Solo game play: Play the daily solo mini crossword once per day!
 - Viewing personal stats: View your stats from fastest time to streaks for both battle and solo games.
 - Inviting a friend to play: A unique link is generated so you can invite a friend to battle!
 - Battle play: See your opponents progress as you battle it out to see who can finish the daily battle crossword first.

## How to Play

### Getting Started
- Navigate to [crosswars.xyz](crosswars.xyz) 
- On your first visit, sign up by entering your name, email, and creating a password.
   - You'll have to validate your email before playing.
   - On subsequent visits, log in with the same email and password.
   - You can also play as a guest but then you can't battle or see your stats :(

### Solo Play
- Select **Solo** from your dashboard
- Complete the daily mini-crossword at your own pace
- View your personal stats including fastest times and streaks

### Battle Play (Racing Friends)

Battle mode lets you race against a friend on the same crossword in real-time!

**Starting a Battle (Inviter):**
1. Click **Battle Play** from your dashboard (requires login)
2. Copy the invite link and send it to your friend
3. Enter the Battle Room and wait for them to join
   - They have until the end of the day to accept
   - They can play as guest or logged-in user

**Joining a Battle (Invitee):**
1. Click the invite link your friend sent you
2. Log in or play as guest
3. You'll join the waiting room with your friend

**Race Gameplay:**
- Once both players hit **Ready**, the crossword appears and timers start
- See your opponent's position via an opaque highlight
- When they complete a word, the highlight becomes bold and their progress updates
- First to finish wins!
- Stats update with your win/loss and completion time

## View Stats
- Select **Stats** from your dashboard
- Checkout your solo game and battle game stats.
