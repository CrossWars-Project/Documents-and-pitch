# Racing Friends

## What is Racing a Friend?

- Both players will get the same mini and have respective time clocks to see who finishes first (and wins)

- You will be able to see where your opponent is working and when they have gotten a word, but not what their answers or attempts are

    - You will be able to see where your friend is working via an opaque highlight on that section. 

    - When your opponent gets a word, the highlight will become bold. 

- At the end of the game, your stats will be updated with either a win or a loss and how long it took you to complete the crossword (if you were able to finish)



## How to Race a Friend

**If you are the inviter, start here:** 

- After you log in press **Battle Mode** instead of solo

    - You must have an account to invite a friend to race!

- Copy the provided link and send it to a Friend, then enter the battle room

    - Your friend has until the end of the day to accept the invite

    - Your friend can play as a guest or a logged-in user

    - Until your friend accepts you, you will not be in waiting; at this point, you will not be able to see the crossword.

- Once your Friend accepts, you will be notified, and they will be added to the same waiting room.

    - If the invite expires or is declined, you will be notified and ejected from the waiting room and have the option of playing solo or inviting someone else

    - You can only play one solo and one race mode a day



**If you are the invitee, start here:**

- Click the link your friend sent you.

- You will be prompted to log in or play as a guest

- You will be added to the same waiting room as your friend

    - They will be notified that you have joined 



- Once both players are actively in the waiting room and hit the **Ready to Race** button, after a countdown from 3, the crosswords will be revealed to both players, and your respective clocks will start

- Race until one of you wins!
